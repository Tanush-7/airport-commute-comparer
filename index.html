5 hacks Bengaluru regulars use

From frequent flyers in Whitefield, HSR and Indiranagar — not first-timers.

1
Book Shoffr / scheduled cabs for 4–6 AM flights

App cabs cancel most at 3–5 AM. A pre-booked airport specialist with a driver assigned the night before is worth ₹200 extra at that hour.

2
Take Vajra to Hebbal, cab from there — in reverse

Landing at KIA? Vajra out of the airport is fast until Hebbal. Hop off at Hebbal / Esteem Mall and take a 15-min cab home. Saves 40 mins in peak.

3
KIA Halt train is brilliant — if you're near a station

Train fare itself is ₹35 to KIA Halt + free shuttle — total in the comparer is ₹115 for MG Road (₹35 train + ~₹80 to reach KSR station). From KSR, KR Puram or Yelahanka it's often faster than a cab in peak.

4
Add 30 mins only for Hebbal, not the whole trip

Check Google Maps for Hebbal specifically. If Hebbal shows deep red, leave early. If it's green, the airport highway is usually clear.

5
Groups of 3–4: cab always wins on value

4 × Vajra at ₹330 = ₹1,320. A sedan cab is ₹1,150–1,300 total, door-to-door. Solo? Bus wins. Couple with heavy bags? Cab wins on effort.
